Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 24, 2026

Introduction

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use PlaySplit (the "App"). The App helps organize badminton players, create groups, shuffle players into balanced courts, visualize rotations, and track scores.

Information We Collect

Data stored locally: Player names, grade levels, groups, shuffle results, and settlement/score data are stored locally on your device using SQLite. We do not collect or transmit your personal data to our servers.

No account required: The App does not require you to create an account to use its core features.

Device identifiers (advertising): When ads are displayed, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device advertising identifiers (e.g., IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android), IP address, and coarse location data solely for the purpose of serving advertisements. The App requests non-personalized ads only by default. You may reset or limit ad tracking through your device's privacy settings.

Advertising

The App displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, IP addresses, and general usage data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Ads are configured to request non-personalized advertising (requestNonPersonalizedAdsOnly: true). You may opt out of personalized ads through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

How We Use Information

Information is used exclusively to provide and improve the App's functionality — organizing players, balancing shuffle results, rendering court visualizations, and tracking scores/settlements. We do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared with third-party SDKs only as required for their services (e.g., advertising).

Data Storage & Retention

All player, group, and score data is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data. If you uninstall the App, locally stored data will be deleted. If you back up your device to cloud services, those backups are subject to the cloud provider's policies.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — banner advertising (Privacy Policy)
  • Expo / EAS — app build, updates, and distribution (Privacy Policy)

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us to request deletion.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us
