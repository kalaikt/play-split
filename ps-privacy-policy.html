Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 24, 2026

Introduction

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use the App (the "App"). The App helps organize players, create groups, and shuffle players into courts.

Information We Collect

Data stored locally: Player names, optional profile details, groups, and shuffle results are stored locally on your device using SQLite. We do not collect or transmit your personal data to our servers.

No account required: The App does not require you to create an account to use its core features.

Advertising

The App may display advertisements (for example, via Google AdMob). Ad providers may collect and process device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage data as described in their policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party ad providers. Where possible, ads are configured to request non-personalized advertising; you may opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and improve the App's functionality — organizing players, balancing shuffle results, and rendering court visualizations. We do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared with third-party SDKs only as required for their services (e.g., advertising).

Data Storage

All player and group data is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data. If you uninstall the App, locally stored data will be deleted. If you back up your device to cloud services, those backups are subject to the cloud provider's policies.

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services, such as:

  • Google AdMob — for advertising
  • Expo — for app updates and distribution

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us to request deletion.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: info@thulircloud.com
